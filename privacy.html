简体中文

适用范围

本政策适用于 iPhone 和 iPad 上的 MomentyFonts。MomentyFonts 用于书写个人笔迹、保存字体包、预览笔迹，并在完成所选文字系统后生成 TrueType 字体（.ttf）。从 1.1 版本起，创建、书写、编辑、保存、App 内 Preview 和可编辑字体包导入/导出仍然免费；只有通过系统分享面板把完成的 TTF 发送到 App 外时，才需要一次性“永久 TTF 导出”购买。

我们不收集的数据

MomentyFonts 不创建账号，不展示广告，不运行分析，不跨 App 或网站跟踪你，也不会把笔迹、项目、生成的字体、Pressure 数据、购买历史、设备标识符或使用行为发送到 Momenty 运营的服务器。MomentyFonts 没有 Momenty 运营的云端后端。

设备上的数据

字体包、草稿、书写偏好和生成的字体默认保存在 App 的本地容器中。可编辑的 .momentyfont 文件包含保存的字体包数据；生成的 .ttf 文件是用于分享和在支持它的环境中使用的字体文件。两者用途不同。

不可替代的本地书写内容可以随设备设置进入 iCloud 设备备份或电脑备份。可重新生成的构建文件和临时文件不会作为需要长期保留的用户内容处理。备份是否发生由你的 Apple 设备和备份设置决定。

数据何时离开 App

数据会在以下情况下离开 App：你主动使用 Files、AirDrop 或其他系统分享目的地导出或分享 .ttf 或 .momentyfont 文件；符合条件的本地内容按设备设置进入 iCloud 设备备份或电脑备份；从 1.1 版本起，StoreKit 产品加载、购买、权益验证和恢复请求会与 Apple App Store 服务通信。MomentyFonts 不会把笔迹、项目或生成的字体附加到 StoreKit 请求。导出或分享后，目的地服务自己的隐私政策适用。取消分享不会改变 App 内的字体包。

Apple App Store 服务

从 1.1 版本起，MomentyFonts 使用 Apple StoreKit 加载“永久 TTF 导出”产品与本地化价格、完成购买、验证当前权益、接收交易更新，并在用户明确选择 Restore Purchases 时恢复购买。这些操作由 Apple 提供，受 Apple 的隐私政策与 App Store 账号设置约束。MomentyFonts 只在设备上处理 Apple 验证的产品与权益状态，不把该状态发送到 Momenty 服务器。

设备功能的使用

MomentyFonts 使用设备本地偏好设置来保存外观等选择，读取本地文件的时间和属性来检查项目与导入文件，读取系统运行时间来处理短时输入与界面计时，并检查可用磁盘空间以安全完成数据导入或导出。这些用途不会把数据发送给 Momenty。

保留与删除

本地字体包会保留到你在 App 中删除它或删除 App。你保存到 Files、发送给其他 App 或包含在设备备份中的副本不受 App 控制，需要在对应位置单独管理。

联系与变更

隐私问题请通过 MomentyFonts Support 提交。我们会在政策发生变化时同步更新 App 内文字和本公开页面。

English

Scope

This policy covers MomentyFonts on iPhone and iPad. MomentyFonts lets you write personal handwriting, save font packs, preview saved writing, and build a TrueType font (.ttf) after completing the selected writing system. Starting with version 1.1, creating, writing, editing, saving, in-app Preview, and editable font-pack import and export remain free. Only sending a finished TTF outside the app through the system Share Sheet requires the one-time Permanent TTF Export purchase.

Data we do not collect

MomentyFonts does not create accounts, show ads, run analytics, track you across apps or websites, or send handwriting, projects, generated fonts, Pressure data, purchase history, device identifiers, or usage behavior to Momenty-operated servers. MomentyFonts has no Momenty-operated cloud backend.

Data on your device

Font packs, drafts, writing preferences, and generated fonts stay in the app's local container by default. An editable .momentyfont file contains saved font-pack data. A generated .ttf file is a font file for sharing and use in environments that support it. The two files serve different purposes.

Irreplaceable local handwriting content may be included in an iCloud device backup or computer backup according to your device settings. Reproducible build files and temporary files are not treated as user content that needs long-term backup. Your Apple device and backup settings control whether a backup occurs.

When data leaves the app

Data leaves the app when you export or share a .ttf or .momentyfont file through Files, AirDrop, or another system destination; when eligible local content enters an iCloud device backup or computer backup according to your device settings; or, starting with version 1.1, when StoreKit product loading, purchase, entitlement verification, and Restore requests communicate with Apple App Store services. MomentyFonts does not attach handwriting, projects, or generated fonts to StoreKit requests. After export or sharing, the destination service's privacy policy applies. Canceling a share does not change the font pack in the app.

Apple App Store services

Starting with version 1.1, MomentyFonts uses Apple StoreKit to load the Permanent TTF Export product and localized price, make a purchase, verify current entitlement, receive transaction updates, and restore purchases when the user explicitly chooses Restore Purchases. These operations are provided by Apple and are subject to Apple's privacy policy and App Store account settings. MomentyFonts processes Apple-verified product and entitlement status on device and does not send that status to a Momenty server.

Use of device functions

MomentyFonts uses local preferences for choices such as Appearance, reads local file dates and attributes to check projects and selected import files, reads system uptime for short input and presentation timers, and checks available disk space to complete data imports or exports safely. These uses do not send data to Momenty.

Retention and deletion

Local font packs remain until you delete them in the app or delete the app. Copies saved to Files, sent to another app, or included in a device backup are outside the app's control and must be managed at their destination.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, use MomentyFonts Support. If this policy changes, the in-app copy and this public page will be updated together.
