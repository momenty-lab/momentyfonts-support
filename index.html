自然书写，完成自己的字体

在 iPhone 或 iPad 的完整纸张页面上连续书写，预览保存的笔迹，并在完成所选文字系统后生成可分享的 TrueType 字体。

从 1.1 版本起，创建、书写、编辑、保存、App 内预览和可编辑字体包导入/导出仍然免费；只有通过系统分享面板把完成的 TTF 发送到 App 外时，才需要一次性“永久 TTF 导出”购买，价格以 App Store 在当前店面的显示为准。

MomentyFonts 不需要账号，没有广告、分析或跟踪。项目默认保存在设备上。数据会在你主动导出或分享时，或在符合条件的本地内容按设备设置进入系统备份时离开 App；从 1.1 版本起，产品加载、购买和恢复还会与 Apple App Store 服务通信，但不会附带笔迹、项目或生成的字体。

Write naturally. Finish a font of your own.

Write continuously on complete paper pages on iPhone or iPad, preview your saved handwriting, and build a shareable TrueType font after completing the selected writing system.

Starting with version 1.1, creating, writing, editing, saving, in-app Preview, and editable font-pack import and export remain free. Only sending a finished TTF outside the app through the system Share Sheet requires the one-time Permanent TTF Export purchase. The price is the price displayed by the App Store for the current storefront.

MomentyFonts requires no account and includes no advertising, analytics, or tracking. Projects stay on your device by default. Data leaves the app when you export or share it, or when eligible local content enters a system backup according to your device settings. Starting with version 1.1, product loading, purchase, and Restore also communicate with Apple App Store services, without attaching handwriting, projects, or generated fonts.
