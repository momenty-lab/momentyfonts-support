自然书写，完成自己的字体

在 iPhone 或 iPad 的完整纸张页面上连续书写，预览保存的笔迹，并在完成所选文字系统后生成可分享的 TrueType 字体。

MomentyFonts 不需要账号，没有广告、分析或跟踪。项目默认保存在设备上；数据只会在你主动导出或分享时，或在符合条件的本地内容按设备设置进入系统备份时离开 App。

Write naturally. Finish a font of your own.

Write continuously on complete paper pages on iPhone or iPad, preview your saved handwriting, and build a shareable TrueType font after completing the selected writing system.

MomentyFonts requires no account and includes no advertising, analytics, or tracking. Projects stay on your device by default. Data leaves the app only when you export or share it, or when eligible local content enters a system backup according to your device settings.
